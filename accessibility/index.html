Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to learn about our services and request an estimate, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or other assistive technology.

Our commitment

A2Z Construction Services aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA on a2zcs.net. We design and test the site so it can be used with a keyboard alone, read by screen readers, zoomed to 200 percent and viewed in high contrast.

What we have done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA color contrast
  • Every photo that carries meaning has a text description
  • All pages work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline
  • A skip link jumps past the menu to the main content
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are announced to screen readers
  • Animations are turned off for visitors who ask their device to reduce motion
  • Links that open a new tab say so

Third party content

Some links go to sites we do not control, such as our online labor agreement, Facebook and BuildZoom. We cannot guarantee the accessibility of those sites, but we are glad to help you directly.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this site is hard to use, please tell us and we will help you get what you need another way, such as by phone or email. We aim to respond within two business days.
