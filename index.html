FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does A2Z Construction Services provide?

We specialize in demolition, grading and site prep, trucking, hauling and container rental, and labor crews for residential and commercial projects.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Irvine and serve all of Orange County, including Anaheim, Santa Ana, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, Tustin, Lake Forest, Mission Viejo and surrounding areas.

Can you handle demolition, grading and hauling on the same project?

Yes. That is what we do best. One crew with our own excavators, dump trucks and containers takes your site from demolition to a cleared, graded lot, with the debris hauled away.

What container sizes do you rent?

We offer 10-yard and 40-yard containers for construction debris, cleanouts and recycling, for residential, commercial and industrial customers.

Can I hire just a labor crew?

Yes. You can book labor and sign the agreement online (opens in a new tab) or call us to schedule a crew.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. A2Z Construction Services is licensed and insured and has served Orange County since 2009.

How quickly can I get an estimate?

Estimates are free. Call (949) 776-3898 or fill out the form below. We typically respond within a few hours during business hours and follow up with a formal estimate within one business day.
